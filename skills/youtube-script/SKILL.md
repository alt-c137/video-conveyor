# YouTube script craft (conveyor channels)

Context: channel settings in `conveyor/channels/<id>/channel.json`, rules in `conveyor/prompts/rules.md`,
the conveyor's own prompts `prompts/script.md` / `check.md` / `fix.md`. Output format the conveyor parses:
`===TITLE===`, `===SCRIPT===` with `[SECTION: name]` blocks, `===SELFCHECK===`.

## 1. Before writing — answer in one line each
- Viewer question: what does the viewer type/think that this video answers?
- Promise: what will they be able to do/see differently at the end?
- Stakes: what does the problem cost them (years, money, friendships, health, self-respect)?
- Open loop: one question planted in the hook and answered only in the final minute.
- Motif: 2-4 concrete images that return (a backpack of stones, a spotlight, a magnifying glass…) — they become images and animations.

## 2. Hook (0-15 s) — the most important 40 words
- Start inside a concrete moment of the viewer's life ("You're 40 minutes into a movie you hate…").
- Then the twist / hidden cost / counter-intuitive claim. Extend the title's curiosity, never repeat the title.
- No greetings, no "in this video", no channel name in the first sentence.
- Test: if the first two sentences were a tweet, would someone stop scrolling?
- Pattern of the competitor outliers (Nous Psychology, The Gen X Way, PsychToons; 200k-800k views on tiny channels):
  1) present-tense scene, 3-5 sensory details the viewer recognises ("Grease under his fingernails that will not fully
  wash out"); 2) reject the obvious label ("This is not stubbornness. Not exactly."); 3) promise something deeper;
  4) go straight into content.
- NO welcome / channel intro / subscribe / "by the end you will know 1, 2, 3" in the first 3 minutes. Lesson
  2026-10-29: Brand 01 lost viewers at 0:30-1:10 exactly on a 25 s welcome+agenda block. Subscribe ask only after the
  first payoff (>= minute 3) and once at the end.
- Frame the topic as identity, not lecture: "The Psychology of People Who…" — defend and respect the viewer
  (frugal, quiet, fixes things himself), never mock him.

## 3. Body structure
- 3-5 sections. Each: example → mechanism (study: who, year, what they did, what they found) → how it breaks life →
  mini-conclusion → micro-cliffhanger into the next section.
- Re-hook every ~3 minutes: surprising fact, direct question, or change of scale.
- DEPTH (user, 2026-10-29): when the title promises N items (5 cars, 3 reasons), each item is a full 200-300-word mini-story:
  scene with one person -> why (mechanism/study) -> what it gives and costs -> a twist -> handover. Never a 3-sentence sketch.
- Length: never under 10 min of final video, aim 12-14 (Brand) / 13-15 (Logic). Re-read your own draft for interest and depth.
- Connect with "but" / "so" (tension, consequence), not "and then".
- Specific > generic. Numbers only real ones. One honest hedge for disputed science, then speak confidently.
- Practical exit (channel promise): 2-4 steps doable today, phrased as questions or rules.
- Ending: answer the open loop, call back to the hook image, one-line conclusion, soft CTA, name the next video.

## 4. Voice & visuals
Sentences ≤ 28 words (mostly 8-15). Spoken English, "you". No brackets, lists, symbols, stage directions.
Every sentence must be drawable as a flat cartoon (objects, places, actions). Avoid abstract stacks of nouns.

## 5. Absolute filters (fail = rewrite, never "soften")
- Aqidah: nothing implying the universe/fate/karma/luck decides; no manifesting, astrology, zodiac, tarot, magic,
  talismans, numerology; no mocking religion or believers; responsibility stays with the person.
- Halal: no alcohol, drugs, gambling/betting, riba/loans/credit/interest, speculative trading, dating/romance examples,
  music as an ideal, profanity, sexualised content. Use study/work/projects/sport/family/friends/halal money examples.
- Facts: no invented studies or numbers; researcher + year correct; disputed effects hedged
  (Dunning-Kruger artefact debate, ego depletion replication failure, power posing, etc.).

## 6. Self-review checklist (write it into ===SELFCHECK===)
Hook grabs in 2 sentences? Open loop paid off? Re-hooks every ~3 min? No repeated thought? Every study verifiable?
Hedges in place? Word count = target ±12 %? Filters passed? Each section ends with a pull forward?

## 7. Titles
Clickable but honest, ≤ 60 chars, viewer-centric ("Why You…", "Nobody Tells You…", "The … That …"). Always also a plain
searchable safe_title. Never promise what the script does not deliver.
